Android · Rodalies, FGC, Metro, TRAM and buses

Is your train running late? Check before you reach the station.

MUR – Mobilitat Urbana i Rodalies brings Catalonia’s public transport together in one app: Rodalies, FGC, TMB Metro, TRAM and buses. It is built for quick lookups, with real-time data and no fuss: open the app, check your station and decide whether to wait, run or change plans.

Get it on Google Play Data sources

What you can do

Timetables and stations
See the next departures by station or by line, with the delay and platform when the operator provides them. Save your favourite stations, lines and buses to keep them at hand.
Geotren
A vector map with trains on the move, stops and customisable layers so you only see what matters to you.
Journeys
Search journeys, find the nearest station and follow the walking route to the station or stop.
Incidents and alerts
Incidents by line and operators’ posts. With Premium, arrival alerts (the app notifies you only when you are within the distance you choose from the station) and incident alerts.
Widgets and themes
A customisable next-departures widget, also for your nearest station, and themes inspired by each operator (Premium).
Statistics
Punctuality, passenger flow and line status.

Networks and services

Rodalies de Catalunya, FGC, Barcelona Metro (TMB), TRAM Barcelona (Trambaix and Trambesòs) and urban, metropolitan and intercity buses.

Available in Catalan, Spanish, English and French.

MUR Premium (optional)

Remove ads and unlock arrival and incident alerts, the widget and interface themes. You can activate it with a Google Play subscription (monthly or yearly) or with a redeemable code. If you like, you can link Premium to your Google account to get it back when you change phone. Signing in is optional and you can delete your account from within the app.

Your privacy, first

  • No third-party analytics or crash reporting.
  • We don’t sell your data.
  • Settings and favourites stay on your phone.
  • Location is only used if you grant permission and is processed on your device: it is not sent to any server of ours.
  • Without Premium, Google AdMob ads are shown, with a consent form in the EU and the UK.

Android 8.0 or higher. An Internet connection is needed to get real-time data.

Important notice. MUR is an independent project and is not affiliated with Renfe, Rodalies de Catalunya, FGC, TMB, TRAM or any other operator; it uses their open, public data. Real-time information is indicative: confirm important details with the operator. Names and trademarks belong to their owners.
